Omlette Recipe

Here is my favourite omlette recipe!

Ingredients:

  • 3 eggs
  • a pinch of salt
  • a dash of milk
  • 1 tablespoon of butter
  • a handleful of grated cheese
  • toppings of your choosing

Instructions:

  1. crack all the eggs into a bowl and a bit of watter and milk, mix well
  2. add salt and butter and a pinch of cheese, mix well
  3. heat a pan to medium heat and put a dash of oil
  4. pour the mixture into the pan
  5. cook for a few minutes, you will see the edges turn golden brown
  6. add the cheese and fold the omlette in half
  7. serve hot with your favorite toppings
