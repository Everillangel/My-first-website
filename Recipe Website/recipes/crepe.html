Crepes Recipe

Here is my favourite crepes recipe!

Ingredients:

  • 3 large eggs
  • 1 Cup of flour
  • 2 Cup of milk
  • 1 table spoon of butter
  • 1 teaspoon of vanilla extract

Instructions:

  1. Mix together, large eggs, milk, vanilla and butter till well combined
  2. Add the flour and mix until smooth, thin with water if needed
  3. Let it rest for 10 to 20 minutes, this is optional
  4. Heat a pan on medium heat and add some oil to coat it lightly
  5. Pour about a half cup of batter to the pan and swirl it until the bottom is covered, If it is too little or too much, adjust accordingly
  6. cook until loose and flip, it is done when both sides are golden brown
